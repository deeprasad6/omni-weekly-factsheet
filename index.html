Metrics & Customer Insights Customer journey · VOC & sentiment · ELM campaigns · Performance metrics · Sources: Chase canvas 6/6 · Scorecard · ~850 verbatims
Strategic read: Customers are not rejecting Omni — they are rejecting moments where Omni breaks financial truth. Every major complaint maps to a single failure mode: Omni asserting something that contradicts what the customer can see or knows to be true in their own books. CSAT and sentiment are improving because V2 is more accurate and faster, but "no data found when data clearly exists" remains the single largest trust-breaker and must be treated as a product correctness issue, not a UX issue.
Customer Journey — Where Trust Is Built and Lost
How to read this
Six stages from discovery to loyalty. The metrics row below each stage shows the program signals that tell us how Omni is performing at that moment. Opt-out demand is the clearest V2 win at Stage 2: customers who hit early friction demanded to turn Omni off — 40% at launch, now 9%. That single trend shows V2 improved first contact. The job now is to fix the accuracy and trust failures at Stages 3–4 so more customers reach the loyalty cohort at Stage 5.
